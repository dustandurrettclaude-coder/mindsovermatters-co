OPEN HOUSE KIT — 5 PRINTABLE SHEETS
===================================

WHAT'S INSIDE
1. Open House Sign-In Sheet (standard) — 14 rows, captures agent status and
   pre-approval so you know who is a real buyer before you follow up.
2. Open House Sign-In Sheet (20 rows) — same thing, tighter rows, for busy days.
3. Visitor Feedback Form — hand to serious visitors. The pricing question gives
   your seller real evidence when it is time to talk about a price change.
4. Follow-Up Tracker — the one most agents skip. Transfer names the same evening
   and log the DATE of each touch, not a checkmark.
5. Property Feature Sheet — a clean one-page leave-behind.

HOW TO USE
- Print on US Letter (8.5 x 11). No scaling, no "fit to page".
- Black and white prints fine. The navy bar prints as grey.
- Print the sign-in sheet double-sided with the feature sheet to save paper.

CAN I EDIT THESE?
These are print-and-write PDFs, designed to be used with a pen at the door.
If you want your name and brokerage printed on every sheet instead of
handwritten, reply to your order and ask — it is a quick change.

TERMS
Personal and single-agent commercial use. Print as many copies as you like for
your own listings. Please do not resell or redistribute the files themselves.

Not legal advice. Check your brokerage's policy on collecting visitor contact
information and any state rules that apply to you.
