LANDLORD FORMS & CHECKLISTS — 8 PRINTABLE SHEETS
================================================
1. Rent-Ready Turnover Checklist     5. Rent Payment Ledger (12 months)
2. Move-In Condition Report          6. Tenant Application Tracker
3. Move-Out Condition Report         7. Annual Inspection Checklist
4. Maintenance Request Log           8. Vendor & Contractor Sheet

HOW TO USE
- Print on US Letter (8.5 x 11) at 100%, no scaling. Black and white is fine.
- The two condition reports are designed to be compared line by line. Fill in the
  move-in report with the tenant present and photograph anything below "good".
- The maintenance log and rent ledger are the records you will want if a dispute
  ever starts. One line per event, filled in the day it happens.

WHAT IS NOT IN HERE, ON PURPOSE
No lease, no notice to vacate, no eviction paperwork. Those are legal documents
whose required wording changes by state and often by city, and a generic version
can cost you a case. Get those from your state REALTOR association, a local
landlord association, or an attorney.

TERMS
Personal and single-owner commercial use. Print as many copies as you like for
your own properties. Please do not resell or redistribute the files.

Not legal advice. Security-deposit rules, notice periods, inspection rights and
fair-housing requirements vary by state and city. Check yours.
