Annual Inspection

Once a year, with proper notice to the tenant. Catching small things here is what keeps turnovers cheap.

Safety

Smoke detectors sound when tested
CO detector present and working
Fire extinguisher charged, if provided
Exits clear and doors operate
No visible extension-cord daisy chains
Handrails secure
Water heater strapped where required

Water and moisture

No leaks under sinks
No staining on ceilings
Caulk intact at tub and shower
No standing water at foundation
Gutters flowing away from the house
No musty smell in closets or basement
Toilet bases dry

Systems

HVAC filter clean and dated
Vents unobstructed
Dryer vent clear of lint
Breaker panel accessible
No hot outlets or cover plates missing
Sump pump runs, if present

Exterior and lease compliance

Roof line and flashing look sound
Siding and trim intact
Landscaping maintained per lease
Approved occupants only
Pets match what was approved
No unapproved alterations
Parking used as agreed
