BUYER & SELLER TRANSACTION CHECKLISTS — 7 PRINTABLE SHEETS
==========================================================
1. Buyer Consultation              5. Showing Feedback Log
2. Contract to Close Timeline      6. Closing Document Checklist
3. Listing Appointment Checklist   7. Transaction Contact Sheet
4. Pre-Listing Prep (give this one to your seller)

HOW TO USE
- Print on US Letter (8.5 x 11) at 100%, no scaling.
- Sheet 2 is the one that saves deals. The day a contract is signed, write the
  actual calendar date next to every deadline on it.
- Sheet 4 is written to be handed to a seller as-is.
- Sheet 5 exists so that when three showings give the same objection, you have it
  in writing for the pricing conversation.

TERMS
Personal and single-agent commercial use. Print as many copies as you like for
your own transactions. Please do not resell or redistribute the files.

Not legal advice. Contract deadlines, disclosure requirements and agency rules
vary by state and by brokerage. Follow your own contract and broker policy.
