Pre-Listing Prep

Hand this to your seller. Everything here is cheap and moves the photographs.

Outside

Mow, edge and trim back from the house
Fresh mulch at the beds
Clear the porch to two clean items
Wash the front door and polish hardware
Clean the windows, at least the front
Pressure-wash walk and driveway
Store bins, hoses and toys out of sight
Park cars off the driveway for photos

Every room

Remove about half of what is on surfaces
Take down personal photos
Take everything off the refrigerator
Match the light bulbs, all on, all working
Open every blind for photos
Remove or store pet bowls and beds
Shoes, coats and bags out of entryways

Kitchen and baths

Counters cleared to two or three items
Sink empty and dry
Fresh white towels
Toilet lids down
Shampoo and razors out of the shower
Trash cans emptied and hidden

Before every showing

Lights on, ceiling fans off
Temperature comfortable
Music off, television off
Beds made, laundry away
Valuables and medications secured
Pets and pet smells handled
Leave the house, and take the pets
