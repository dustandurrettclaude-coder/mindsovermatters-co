Listing Appointment

What to bring, what to ask, and what to leave behind.

Before you go

Pull tax record and prior sale
Run comparable sales
Check for liens or permits
Drive the street and note the approach
Prepare net-proceeds estimate
Print listing paperwork

At the table

Tour the home and take notes
Ask why they are selling and when
Ask what they owe, roughly
Ask if anyone else is competing for the listing
Walk through your pricing evidence
Explain your marketing plan
Explain commission and who pays what
Ask for the listing

Property facts to capture

Year built and major updates with dates
Roof, HVAC, water heater ages
Known defects and past repairs
HOA dues and what they cover
Utilities and average costs
Survey, warranties, manuals
What stays and what goes
Occupancy and showing instructions

Before you leave

Listing agreement signed
Disclosures given to seller
Photography scheduled
Sign and lockbox arranged
Pre-listing prep list left behind
Next contact date agreed
