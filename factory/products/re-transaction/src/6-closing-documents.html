Closing Documents

Collect these as you go, not the night before.

In the file

Fully executed purchase contract
All addenda and amendments, signed
Agency disclosures
Seller property disclosure
Lead-based paint disclosure if pre-1978
Earnest money receipt
Inspection report and repair agreement
Repair invoices and receipts
Appraisal
Title commitment
Survey
HOA documents and estoppel
Home warranty
Wire instructions, verified by phone
Closing disclosure
Final walkthrough acknowledgment
Verify every wire instruction by calling a number you already had, never a number from the email. Wire fraud in real estate targets exactly this moment.
