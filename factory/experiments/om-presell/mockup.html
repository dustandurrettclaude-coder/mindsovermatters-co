Executive Summary

A stabilized, multi-tenant asset in a growth corridor

[Brokerage] is pleased to present the exclusive opportunity to acquire The Pinehurst Professional Center, a 42,300-square-foot, two-building professional office campus located along the Commerce Way corridor in Ocala, Florida.

The property is 94% leased to eleven tenants with a weighted average remaining lease term of 4.2 years, anchored by a regional medical group occupying 31% of the rentable area through 2031. In-place rents sit roughly 8% below the submarket average, offering measurable upside at rollover.

Recent capital improvements include a new TPO roof (2024), LED lighting retrofit, and resurfaced parking, positioning the asset for a low-capex hold period.
