MINDS OVER MATTERS
Mind over chatter.
Remember · Organize · Plan · Execute · See it all · Prove

You've had this happen. You spend an hour with Claude working something out. Tomorrow
you open a new chat and it's gone — or it "remembers" a few facts about you but has no
idea what was open, what you'd parked, or what you promised to do yourself. So you
re-explain. Again.

Claude's memory is real, and it's good at what it does: it remembers things about you.
What it doesn't do is run your work. It never holds a goal open until it's finished. It
never hands your next conversation a brief. It never shows you everything in one place
so you can see, in ten seconds, where you actually stand.

That's what Minds Over Matters does.

IT REMEMBERS YOUR WORK.
Not just facts about you — the work itself. Every session opens with your open goals and
last decisions already loaded. Close it and it writes the record, pulls out the lessons,
and leaves a brief for next time. No chat ever starts from zero. Keep it in mind.
Literally.

IT CONSOLIDATES IT.
Every open item gets checked against real evidence — a query, a file that should exist,
a box only you can tick. What's proven gets closed. What's unfinished gets carried into
your next session. What was abandoned gets parked with its own finish line. What's
resolved gets cleared — never deleted, so nothing is ever lost, just settled. Never lose
your mind. Or your place.

IT ORGANIZES IT.
Goals in one place, sorted by the areas you actually work in. Too big for today? Park it
— it comes back when you're ready. The steps only you can take — a click, an upload, a
decision — go on a tracker Claude keeps showing you until they're genuinely done.

IT PLANS FROM WHAT IT ALREADY KNOWS.
Because the memory is there, planning starts from your real situation, not a blank page.
Scope a task before you build it. Refine a plan until the holes are gone. Workflows
you've run before come back as an offer when a session looks like one — never forced on
you.

IT EXECUTES WITH MORE THAN ONE PAIR OF HANDS.
Fan a big job out to parallel agents that each come back with conclusions, not
transcripts. The right model gets picked for each piece of work — heavy thinking where
it matters, cheap and fast where it doesn't.
[qualify by surface — see gate 5]

YOU CAN SEE ALL OF IT — AND START FROM IT.
One dashboard, built straight from your own database, shows everything in the mind:
every goal, every parked thread, every step waiting on you, every saved workflow, every
skill in plain English. Open it in the morning and review the whole board in a glance.
Then pick up where you left off — the dashboard hands you the line that starts your next
session with full context, so continuity is a click, not a memory test. A state of mind
you can actually see.
[confirm kickoff-from-dashboard — see gate 6]

AND "DONE" MEANS SOMETHING.
Every goal carries a check, and it closes when the check passes — not when Claude says
so. You'll never find out three weeks later that "done" meant "I wrote a paragraph about
it." Peace of mind, with receipts.

IT'S YOURS.
The whole record lives in a free database account you own. Export it in one command, any
time. No vendor is holding your work.

WHY THIS AND NOT SOMETHING FREE
Plenty of free tools give Claude a better memory, and some are excellent. Remembering is
the easy part. None of them consolidate the work, plan from it, run it with parallel
agents, put the whole picture on one board you can review in seconds, or hold a goal
open until it's proved. That's the difference between a chat that feels productive and
work that actually gets finished.
